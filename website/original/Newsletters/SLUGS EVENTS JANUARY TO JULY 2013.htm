SLUGS EVENTS JANUARY TO JULY 2013

19th/20th January:   Walking in Borrowdale

It’s always nice to get away to the Lakes in Winter, so we’re having another weekend based at the Fell and Rock hut in Rosthwaite. Contact Anne and Ken if you want to come, so we can book some beds.

Ken and Anne (01200 425841) daykin@talktalk.net

 

Sunday 3rd February:   Caving in the Dales

Relive your lost youth with a trip down Calf Holes. (We cycled past recently, and got quite excited!) Start at Bernie’s at 10.30 if you want the full nostalgia kick (or want to hire a light and helmet from Inglesport). The plan is to drive up to the entrance for 12 noon. Bring a harness and descender for the short entrance pitch if you still have them.

Contact Jes Peterson (01543 360607) jerry.peterson@dudley.gov.uk

 

2/3rd March:   Birthday Special!

Dave Brown turns 60 (yes, 60) at the end of February, so he’s arranged a birthday weekend at John’s house in Riddlesden, where there is plenty of space for fun and frolics and options for sleeping indoors or out. Come for the weekend, or at the very least come for the party on Saturday night. Not to be missed!

Dave Brown (01484 680526) gou491@aol.com  John Boardman (01535 681863) jaybee258@hotmail.com

 

23rd /24th March:   Mountain Biking at LLandegla

Mountain biking in the forest, with trails at all grades. You are invited to Howard and Jo’s for Saturday night. Howard says: “5 people get a bed after that it is camping or sharing with the horses or chickens” so let him know! LLandegla is about 10 miles north of Llangollen, H+J’s place house is at Froncysyllte, about 5 miles east of Llangollen. (Llangollen is near Wrexham!)

Howard Jones (07917197912) howardjjones@hotmail.co.uk   biking centre:  www.coedllandegla.com

Campsite phone: (01690 720228)  www.dolgam-snowdonia.co.uk  Ken and Anne (01200 425841)
