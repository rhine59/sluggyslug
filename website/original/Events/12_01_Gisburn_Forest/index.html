Society Of Lancaster University Graduate Speleologists

Gisburn Forest January 2012

With snow on the ground and more than a nip in the air there was some speculation about what the trails might be like. It turned out to be OK with a bit of care. High up the snow was quite grippy. There were "thrills and spills", especially off the logs and one or two icy sections but we had a good day. Nice weather, good views, good fun and a good turn out!
