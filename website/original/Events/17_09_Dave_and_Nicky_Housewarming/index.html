16th – 17th September Dave and Nicky’s housewarming

Not on the official meets list, but a great turn-out on Saturday night to help Dave and Nicky celebrate their recent move. Phil and Elaine even turned up on Saturday and went home again Sunday, all the way from Bristol! On Sunday there was a bit of coming and going, then a little posse of us went for a walk to explore the local environs. The new house is in a fine location on the moors above Keighley, and we wish Dave and Nicky a very happy time there.
