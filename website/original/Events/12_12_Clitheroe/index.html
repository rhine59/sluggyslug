Slugs in Clitheroe, 1st - 2nd December

It was billed as a biking weekend, but the group who assembled at Anne and Ken's on Saturday morning voted with their feet, quite literally. In the absence of bicycles we went for a walk. Sue and George, Pete and Meg, Jes and John and Chris joined us for a stroll up Pendle Hill on a beautiful winter's day.
 
An equally nice Sunday and five of us headed up the hills on the opposite side of the Ribble Valley: Parlick and Fair Snape.
 
It turned out to be a really pleasant and relaxed weekend with good walking, good weather and good company.
