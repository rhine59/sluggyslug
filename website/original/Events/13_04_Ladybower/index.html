Ladybower, April 2013

This is Ladybower.

Page 63

Four gathered, not paying £5 parking, in a layby

Three climbs and three descents, one tumble.

Some sun some rain, some sweat and some grime

Tired but happy at the end coffee in the rain.

Anne, Ken, Jes, Chris Browning.
