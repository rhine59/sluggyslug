Cave Radio and Electronics Group   (and, unobviously, Surveying)
--------------------------------

We aim to encourage the development and use of radio communications and other
electronic and computer equipment in caving and related activities. All
members receive a quarterly _Newsheet_ containing news and letters, and
optionally _Compass Points_, our surveying newsletter which covers all aspects
of cave surveying. We also publish a quarterly _Journal_ of araound 28 pages
covering radio and electronics topics, and organise field meets around the
UK.

Annual membership is #2.50 (or #1.60 for BCRA members)

Journal subscription is an additional #6.00

An index to the journal is available, and various other publications
including our Bibliography of Undergound Communications. Compass Points only
is also available on E-mail. For an order form and information sheet please
email me (Wookey@aleph1.co.uk) your postal address, or write to 

David Gibson, 12 Well House drive, LEEDS, LS8 4BX, UK
