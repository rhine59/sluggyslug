  1. Prologue

    2. During August 1994 five members of LUSS travelled to the Asturias region of northern Spain. In particular the Andara area in Picos De Europas. Our aim was to continue cave exploration carried out by the society in previous years.

  2. Background

    3. The society have been visiting the area since 1971, but it was in 1975 that the first major discovery was made; Cueva Del Agua (Water Cave) a resurgence in the Urdon Gorge near the village of Tresviso. As this cave was extended over a number of years by exploration, LUSS began to look for a top entrance 1700 metres above in the Mazzarazza region. Such a vertical interval might yeald a cave of great importance/depth. During the search for this elusive top entrance many impressive vertical caves were explored. Sara, Tere, Flower Pot and possibly most famous Sima 56.

  3. Objectives

    4. We had two expedition objectives:
    1. Continue to explore for surface shafts in a region we knew from records that previous expeditions had largely ignored.
    2. Investigate The Cheese Cave, a cave previously explored by LUSS which due to its proximity with Sima 56 holds potential for being a "middle entrance" to said cave.

  6. Tips For Caving In Northern Spain

    • Carbide can be bought from hardware stores in large towns. It seems it is used for lighting.
    • Don't take groceries etc with you, surprisingly enough the spanish eat and wipe their bottoms as well.
    • The available maps are not up to the standard of engligh O/S maps. Occasionally we would find on taking a bearing during prospecting that a peak appeared to be on the wrong side of the line!
