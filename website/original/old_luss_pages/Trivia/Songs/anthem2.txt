                        Anthem 2

In north Ribbledale, 'neath limestone and shale,
of potholes there are quite a lot.
But the queen of them all is Niagara Falls,
in the bowels of Pen-y-ghent Pot.
Below Pen-y-ghent Pot,
the pennines are covered in shite.
It's always the same when you mention the name,
the C.R.O. run off in fright.
So this is my story, this is my song,
I've been down this pothole too f__king long.
So roll up your ladders, lifeline and belay,
and we'll come back to Pen-y-ghent Pot some other day.
Up the pitch, up the pitch,
and away, and away,
and we'll come back to Pen-y-ghent Pot some other day.
