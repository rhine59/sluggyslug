                    Caving Matilda

Once a jolly caver camped by a smallet hole,
under the shade of a rowan tree,
and he sang as he tied his ladder to a stalagmite,
who'll come a caving Matilda with me.

Caving Matilda, caving Matilda,
who'll come a caving Matilda with me,
and he sang as he tied his ladder to a stalamite,
who'll come a caving Matilda with me.

Deep beneath the surface, far inside a bedding plane,
where oh where can the through route be?
and he sang as he heard the murmer of a waterfall,
who'll come a caving Matilda with me.

Caving Matilda.....

Up popped a cloud burst, flooded down that swallet hole,
down to that bedding plane turf brown sea,
and he sang as the water flowed into that bedding plane,
don't come a caving Matilda with me.

Caving Matilda.....

Up drove the rescue, seated in an ambulance,
out jumped the wardens, ONE, TWO, THREE!
and they sang as they sat and waited for the sun to shine,
who'll come a caving Matilda with me.

Caving Matilda.....

Deep beneath the surface, far inside that bedding plane,
there lies a caver never to be seen,
and his voice may be heard as you pass by that swallet hole,
don't come a caving Matilda with me.

Caving Matilda.....
