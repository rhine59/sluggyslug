Dundee Satellite Receiving Station

These links provide access to just the most recent of the pictures at the Dundee Satellite Receiving station. They are in JPEG format. Further information about these images, more images and how to purchase high resolution versions can be found on their other pages.
  • Channel One - Daytime cloud/surface mapping.
  • Channel Two - Surface water delineation, ice and snow melt.
  • Quick Look Thumbnail Browsing - A Form which allows retreival of a page with several images reflecting the weather over the UK as it changed in the last few hours.
