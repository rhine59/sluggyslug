P5 Shakehole Dig - Shrinking Pot

Dig at P5 sink. (Shrinking Pot) In the middle 1980's, when LUSS were busy pushing P5 to its limit much attention was paid to the possibility of digging in the centre of the P5 sinkhole because it lay directly above Pinnacle Aven in Grange Rigg. However, at the time it was decided that such a project was beyond the enginnering capabilities of the club. In 1997 a group of LUSS cavers were intending to descend Christmas Pot, however, a quick stop at P5 to fill our carbides revealed that part of the cliff face had collapsed. Phil and Jeremy began moving some of the larger rocks to reveal the water sinking down the narrowest of cracks. Joined by Geoff we set about damming the stream and moving somemore rock. The end of the day saw a tantalising crack with the water flowing down it and to one side what looked like the start of a crawl that turned sharply right. The following week saw Phil, Jeremy, Geoff, Derek and Rachel back at the dig. The crack had by now blocked up, so attention was turned to the 'crawl'. Phil lay in the crawl, half submerged for 1 and a half hours only to find that Geoff had a wetsuit on all along. However, the day resulted in the shifting of a lot of flood debris and rocks from the crawl. Phil, Neil and Andy moved out lots of rocks from the crawl to reveal it was about three foot full of crap. The next week saw Phil, Derek, Cass and Geoff moving more rock. However, the crawl had come to nothing and we were proceeding to dig downwards. A weekend trip saw Phil, Derek and Jeremy completely destroying the dig by smashing most of the rock out the way (hence the name Shrinking Pot). At first this seemed to make it all very unstable, but the remaining roof continues to hold out. The dig is now about 10 ft lower than when I was laying in the 'crawl', and there are now numerous tantalising drafts and a possible very tight crawl. 6/7/97 - Well a tantalising passage was found heading under the sink but the roof was completely unstable by the end of the day we had knocked a huge boulder back into the dig which will need destroying and then we diverted the stream down the dig in an effort to clear some of the rubble out. August 1997 CPC Winch Meet. On a trip to Wades we stopped at P5 to retrieve the bag of digging materials. The dig has now been transformed. The stream had filled up the dig with about two foot of mud, covering the collapsed boulder. However at the same time the stream had cut back around four foot revealing another draughting hole. Removing some stones revealed a toad that jumped down the hole and disappeared forever. Further digging revealed a welly size hole taking all of the P5 stream. Ramming the crowbar into the hole revealed very little resistance and the crowbar could be moved freely about. We decided to leave the stream to work on the dig and hopefully cut back some more, to allow easier access. This hole seems far more promising than the other draughts but could unfortunately just be another split in the cliff face. If not it is most likely to head straight down 15m into Pinnacle Aven.
