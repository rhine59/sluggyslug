1998 OBJECTIVES.


Cueva del Agua.

Gavin Newman et al have continued work in Agua in the main sumps but since 1987 no one has seroiusly pushed the furthest reaches ? (is this true, email Gavin etc.) SWCC discovered the current furthest point Teeth of Satan - Twilight Zone - Death Race 2000, in 1986/7.

This area will be the main objective for 1998. Depending on success in the Sierra del a Corta this will involve underground camping and possible re-rigging much of the tackle that was left in the system.


Sierra de la Corta.

Work on the Sierra de la Corta will centre around refinding two main shafts that were never descended. Discovered in 1986, T291 and ? (ask Howard) are approx. 45m and 80m shafts respectively that are located around 500m from the known end of Agua. Rediscovery of these would enable easier pushing of Agua without the need for underground camping.

The Cheese Cave will continue to be dug at its furthest point, this would also provide a middle entrance into the Agua-Sima 56 system.

Work on the surface will involve locating all known 'LUSS' caves and any other caves, with GPS's. Such work is intended to enhance the current ongoing WWW project to catalogue LUSS discoveries and possible produce a complete publication of LUSS in Spain 1971 to the present day.
