SLUGS Newsletter – April 2000

Having drawn the short straw, I hope I can follow in the footsteps of the illustrious Dave Brown and his predecessors and provide a feast of activities for the remainder of 2000 and beyond. The big event of the year is of course the take-over of Tresviso village by hordes of Slugs. This is the subject of a separate missive from Chris Baxter, and you can also visit the T2000 website on < [external site unavailable]http://www.silverlime.freeserve.co.uk/tresviso/>, which is updated incessantly.

News:

Allen Williams has got engaged to Cora, and they’ll be spending their honeymoon in Treviso. Chris and Anne had a baby boy on 23 March (un-named at the time of writing). Congratulations to all four.
