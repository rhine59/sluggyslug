Events

Lancaster and district Slugs have started an ancient custom of meeting in the Gregsons Bar, Moor Lane, Lancaster, on the last Friday in the month, 9-ish onwards. Drop in if you’re free and/or in the area. See also 30 November below.

10-11 November - Langdale. The FRCC hut at Raw Head, Great Langdale, is booked for the weekend. Superb walking and climbing. Contact Ken and Anne for details, 01254 883934

Sunday 18 November - Helvellyn. Traverse Helvellyn on bikes in appalling weather! Contact Pete Owens if you’re keen: 01925 263507.

Friday 30 November - DIY slide show at the Gregson Institute, Lancaster. This is in fact a non-slug event organised by Ros Riden (Ellwood), but all Slugs are welcome. Bring up to 12 of your favourite slides, on a (loose) caving theme, for the delight and embarrassment of all. For more details contact Chris Baxter 01524 751975 or Phil 01524 37688; the latter if you want sleeping places.

STOP PRESS: the next two items have changed since the printed newsletter was posted. Please inform anyone you know who is not on e-mail and might be interested.

31 December - New Year’s Eve. Jeanne Holman is hosting the traditional party at Strathyre, Hazel Grove Road, Sutton-in-Craven, N. Yorks, BD20 7QT - contact 01535 635952 for details.

January - Ski trip. A Slugs child-friendly ski trip is being organised for mid-January, although no bookings have been made just yet. Contact John Boardman 0777 5796270 or Jeanne Holman 01535 635952 for details.

22-23 February 2002 - Crianlarich. Postponed from last year because of foot-and-mouth, the Ochils Hut is now booked for a weekend of walking, (winter) climbing, skiing, etc. Contact Ken and Anne, 01254 883934.

More events wanted for spring and summer 2002!

Plans are afoot for a SLUGS dinner in September/October (or some other time), if we can find a suitable venue (Horton-in-Ribblesdale looks promising). Contact Sue I’Anson if you’re interested or can suggest somewhere.
