A return visit to Tresviso is a distinct possibility next year, camping in our usual spot. However, some of us may have to do a bit of caving, to convince the National Parks Authority that we’re a bona fide expedition and therefore allowed to camp within the National Park. More details will follow.

 

50 Years of Caving in the Picos de Europa

Carolyn tells us that plans are underway for an International  Congress for Picos de Europa Speleologists, to coincide with 50 years of exploration. All caving groups that have been involved will be invited to this event. It is expected to take place before the summer caving campaigns get underway, possibly the May bank holiday weekend, 29th April to 2nd May. We’ll circulate more information as it unfolds, but if you’re interested contact Carolyn.   Carolyn Ginnever:  cazz1@hotmail.es
