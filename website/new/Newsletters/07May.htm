16th – 17th June: Camping  at Hay on Wye

New House Farm, Bryngwyn, Rhosgoch, Builth Wells, LD2 3JT   

01497 851671     info@new-house-farm.co.uk

There is also B+B available at the farm for any one who doesn't fancy camping.
There is a good riding stables just ten minutes away,  and a canoe hire place on the Wye. The canoing is easy - canadians or kayaks available. You can hire for a day or 1/2 day, downstream only - the company will come and pick you up and take you back to the starting point. Also walking, climbing, cycling if you prefer.

Bring food for a communal barbecue Saturday night.
Contact Bill and Elen on #### ###### or #### ###### and let them know if you’re planning to come (They’ve reserved the whole campsite, so bring your friends!) and if you’re interested in canoing or riding.
