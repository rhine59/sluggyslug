SLUGS Newsletter – March 2002

News

The scattering of Angus’ ashes, long delayed by the foot-and-mouth restrictions, took place on 24 February on the shore of Loch Tulla, north of Bridge of Orchy. We chose a little promontory with a stand of larch trees, a beautiful and peaceful spot. Grid ref. NN 28054185; stop off and remember him if you’re passing by on your way up to Glencoe and beyond.

A Slugs Annual Dinner has been arranged! See 21 September below. Many more events in diverse locations, for your further enjoyment.

There’s several address/phone/e-mail changes in the attached members list.

Events

14 April - Mountain biking in the Dales or Lakes. John Boardman is arranging a non-epic, user-friendly day out - contact 01535 681863 or 0777 5796270 for details.

26-27 April - Kildale, North York Moors. A camping barn has been booked at Park Farm, Kildale, south of Guisborough (grid ref NZ 602085). Perfect location for walking (Cleveland Way), mountain biking, daffodils, in an area normally unfrequented by Slugs. £4 per person per night - only a few places left for this popular weekend! Contact Jeanne Holman, 01535 635952.

11-12 May - Coach House, Buttermere. A Spring weekend by one of the most beautiful lakes, in FRCC climbing hut at grid ref. NY 185161. Contact Ken and Anne Daykin, 01254 883934

18-19 May - Peak District weekend. Based at Howard and Gill McKee’s house in Buxton, Derbyshire, Saturday/Sunday but you can turn up Friday evening. There's floor space for 8 in the house and about three tents on the front/back lawn, self catering (but they can lay on something basic vegetarian). Lots of activities - climbing/walking/caving/cycling but attendees will have to arrange these themselves. Numbers limited, so contact Howard and Gill if you’re coming. 32 Dovedale Crescent, Buxton, Derbyshire, SK17 9BH. 01298 70542

1 June - Golden Jubilee party (Rosie’s 50th birthday, that is). Food and wine and possibly dancing at 48 Rydal Road, Lancaster, 8 pm until morning. RSVP for bed/floor/tent space: Phil and Rosie, 01524 37688.

22-23 June - Camping at Seathwaite (Stonethwaite ?? Ian E ?), Borrowdale. Celebrate the longest day (almost) in the heart of the Lake District. Camping at grid ref NY 257140. Contact Phil and Rosie 01524 37688.

20-21 July - Camping at Capel Curig, N. Wales. Walking, biking, climbing, canoeing etc. in Snowdonia. Campsite at grid ref SH 743573, on left of A5 between Betws-y-Coed and Capel Curig. Contact Meg & Pete Owens 01925 263507.

24–26 August – Bank Holiday in the Trossachs. Camping at Strathyre campsite off A84, 6 miles north of Callander, grid ref NN 559162. Lots of great family cycling on cycle tracks, Lots of local walks – Stob Binnien, Ben Vorlich, Ben Ledi, Ben An. Steamer trips on Loch Katrine. Last year’s event was enjoyed by all. Contact Meg & Pete Owens 01925 263507.

21 September - SLUGS DINNER! This is the big event, long overdue, celebrating 25 or 26 years of Slugs depending on how good your arithmetic is. We have booked a function room at the Bull i‘th Thorn, about 5 miles south of Buxton, on the A515 Buxton-Ashbourne road. 1st (or 2nd?) pub on the left in the middle of nowhere, about a mile and a half beyond the last of the limestone quarries. There’s camping outside, possibly floor space if we work on them, and B&B in nearby villages. Howard and Gill (see 18-19 May) can put people up and share taxis etc.

Sue I’Anson will be sending round booking forms and menus in due course, but put this in your diaries now and let’s see how many Slugs we can gather in one room. We need to think about entertainments - a Tresviso 2000 slide show? Sketches? (some are writing them already) Songs? Music - we can provide our own.

7-9 March 2003 (provisional). Hostelling at Newtonmore, Scotland. There’s an independent hostel where you can hire cross-country skis. Book yourself in well in advance. Contact Meg & Pete Owens 01925 263507.

Lancaster and district Slugs meet in the Gregsons Bar, Moor Lane, Lancaster, on the last Friday in the month, 9-ish onwards. Drop in if you’re free and/or in the area.
