EVENTS - WINTER '97  - contact listed name or Sue I'Anson (01454 294564) for details


OCTOBER 25TH  SLUGS 21ST BIRTHDAY DINNER
At the Punch Bowl Inn, Low Row, Richmond
If you haven't already sent me your menu choice and money then please do so by the end of
the month. If you have, then call someone you'd like to see there and persuade them to come
as well.  
For more info contact me,  Sue I'Anson 01454 294564

NOVEMBER 2nd   FIREWORKS PARTY AT HOWARD & JO'S
Howard & Jo are warming their new house with a bang! Bonfire party on Saturday night with
fire and food provided. Bring yourself and some fireworks. Lots of space for people to stay. 
Ring Howard Jones or Jo Ellis (01691 773147) for more info and directions to their house. 

NOVEMBER 22TH  DAVE CHECKLEY'S HOUSE WARMING WEEKEND
Spend a cultural Saturday in Manchester visiting the science museum and/or the theatre,
cinema, shopping, with lunch at a restaurant or tapas bar. Then its back to Dave's new house
to sample the delights of  SK9. Sunday's the time for relieving the hangover with a bit of
climbing/cycling/walking or whatever takes your fancy in Derbyshire.
Ring Dave Checkley (01625 528767) for more info and directions to his house. 

JANUARY 16-18 LAKE DISTRICT
Rock and Fell hut in Langdale
Contact Anne or Ken Daykin (01254 883934)  for details.

JANUARY 24 SKIING IN FRANCE
John Boardman will be organising a weeks skiing in France again. Probably for the week from
24th January and probably at Les Arcs, but he's still sorting out the details at the moment.
It'll be organised along the usual lines etc. Last year about 20 people went, which must make
it one of the most popular events on the list!
Contact John Boardman (01904 636518 or 0378 113577)  for more info and to make sure you
reserve your place.
 
MARCH 6-8 CRIANLARICH
The ever popular SLUGS trip to snowy (hopefully) Scotland. As usual there may or may not be
skiing in Glencoe, ice climbing, hill walking, cross country skiing etc. The Ochils club hut
is about half a mile east of the station on the left of the road to Stirling just past the
Ben More bar. Its Annes 40 th birthday on the 3rd , so she may bring some cake and need a
hand celebrating.
Contact Anne or Ken Daykin (01254 883934)  for details.
