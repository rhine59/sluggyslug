Site Guide

In the pages containing picture thumbnails, click on the thumbnail to show a larger version of the picture in the full browser window.

To return to the thumbnails, click on the Back button in your Browser.

If you want to save the pictures on your own computer, then if you right click on any picture, you can "Save Picture As ...", if you right click on any thumbnail, you can "Save Target As ...".

Some pictures have been reduced in size and quality, because of potential problems with web site performance and the amount of free space we can get. These are marked with in the legend with "(R)". If you want copies of the original pictures, then send me a mail, and I'll return them to you as attachments.

The initials of the donor of each picture is shown in the legend (e.g. DW = Dick Willis, work the rest out for yourself).
