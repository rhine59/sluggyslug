                    Drinking Song

As I was walking one fine morning,
in the month of june, by The Jug and the Spoon,
a birdie sat on an ivy bunch,
and the song it sang was a Jug O' Punch.

Too-ra-loo-ra-loo, too-ra-loo-ra-loo,
Too-ra-loo-ra-loo, too-ra-loo-ra-loo,
a birdie sat on an ivy bunch,
and the song it sang was a Jug O' Punch.

What more perversion can a man desire,
than to whip his girl by an open fire,
AKerry pipin th crackle and crunch,
aye and on the table a Jug O' Punch.

Too-ra-loo-ra-loo etc....

Even the doctor with all his art,
cannot cure a man of a broken heart,
Even the cripple forgets his nunch,
When he's safe outside of a Jug O' Punch.

Too-ra-loo-ra-loo etc....

When I'm dead and I'm in my grave,
there is just one thing that I do crave
Just lay me down in my native peat,
with a Jug O' Punch at my head and feet.

Too-ra-loo-ra-loo etc....
