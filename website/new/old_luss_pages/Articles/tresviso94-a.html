LUSS Spanish Caving Expedition Tresviso 94

A Caving Expedition to the Picos De Europe by the members of Lancaster University Speleological Society.

Prologue

During August 1994 five members of LUSS travelled to the Asturias region of northern Spain. In particular the Picos De Europas. Our aim was to continue cave exploration carried out by the society in previous years.

Background

Ninteen seventy five saw the first major exploration/discovery for LUSS in Asturias; Cueva Del Agua (Water Cave) a resurgence in the Urdon Gorge near the village of Tresviso. Since then the club has regularly returned to the region to explore new caves.

Once Cueva Del Agua started to become explored LUSS began looking for a top entrance in the Mazzarazza region which might possibly realise a 1700 metres potential. Many impressive vertical caves were explored. Sara, Tere, Flower Pot and possibly most importantly Sima 56.

Objectives

We had two expedition objectives:
  1. 1.Continue to explore for surface shafts in a region we knew from records that previous expeditions had largely ignored.
  2. 2.Investigate The Cheese Cave, a cave previously explored by LUSS which due to its proximity with Sima 56 holds potential for being a "middle entrance" to said cave.
