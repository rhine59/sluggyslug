Dog Sniffs Out Caver

SLUGS archivePhotosEventsNewslettersSearch

From Dail Express 31st March 1995

Trapped caver Simon Evans waas rescued alive yesterday by Britain's first underground sniffer dog. Labrador Sophie was called in when a six-strong search party failed to find the pot-holer more than 500ft underground. Simon was cut off by a fall of sand in a narrow tunnel and only had enough air to last half an hour after being trapped in the 10-mile Dan-yr-Ogof cave system near Abercrave in mid-Wales.
