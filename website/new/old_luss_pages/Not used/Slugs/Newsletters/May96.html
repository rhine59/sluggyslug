EVENTS - SUMMER '96  - contact listed name or Sue IAnson (01454 294564) for 
details

JUNE 7-9 SWALEDALE MEADOWS
Stroll through the beautiful meadows of Swaledale, full of flowers, or partake 
of some culture on the last weekend of the Swaledale festival. Tour the tea 
shops and pubs studying the art exhibitions  or attend an openair performance 
of The Ring Goes South (from Lord of the Rings) at Bolton Castle.
Camp by the river in Muker near the Miners Arms on the road to Thwaite.
Contact Mary Hatcher for details 01274 582373

JULY 5-7 CAMPING IN THE LAKES 
Camp on the shore of Ullswater at the Waterside House Campsite on the east side 
of the lake GR 463230. Canadian canoes and rowing boats(and maybe mountain 
bikes) for hire. Launching for windsurfers and or just easy  walking or 
spectating  for the land lovers. 
Contact Anne or Ken Daykin for details 01254 883934

AUG 2-4 SLUGS REGATTA, BALA 
Another chance to join the popular sailing and windsurfing event at Bala Race 
across the lake or sunbathe on the shore. Board hire in Bala. River rafting 
near by.
Camping at Llangower Point, South side of lake (GR. 904 323) 

SEPT 13-15 DERBYSHIRE WEEKEND
 Avoid the crowds after the school holidays and go climbing, walking, cycling 
or whatever takes your fancy. Camp behind the George Pub in Alstonefield  GR. 
132555 (site behind the Plough at Hathersage now closed). 
Contact Anne or Ken Daykin for details 01254 883934

OCTOBER 4-6 BRECON WEEKEND
Have a change and head south ! Discover the hills and reservoirs of South Wales 
by foot, bike etc. Those who want to could even organise a caving trip. Camp in 
a field at Talybont-on-Usk GR 117226, near the canal and river with good pubs a 
short stroll away.
Contact Sue IAnson for details 01454 294564
