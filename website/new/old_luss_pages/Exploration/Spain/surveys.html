LUSS in Spain: Surveys of Caves

This is the current list of caves surveys scanned from the journals written after previous expeditions. The surveys are slowly being retraced on computer. Some of the infomation is being lost by this process(my clumsyness) so the original scanned image is also given. The format of the drawing package creates a vector image of the survey. Since this can be used for images that are better than a dot-map a postscript [external site unavailable] (Postscript/?.ps) output is also avialable.

For those with colour screens the images are in colour. Whilst retracing the surveys the colour scheme has evolved. The following is the current list of colours and their associations.

      BlackTitles
      BluePassages(both plan and elevations)
      GreenScales, compasses and anything relating to the way the survey was put onto paper
      MagentaCross-sections
      RedPitches and the corresponding lengths
      Light Blue Water(no distinction is made for sumps)
