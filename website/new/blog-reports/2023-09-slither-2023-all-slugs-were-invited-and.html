 Summer Slither 2023

All Slugs were invited and 27 turned up with Libby for good measure. We ran out of beds and floor space so resorted to tents in the garden.



With sunshine and 25 litres of homemade elderflower champagne and a BBQ in the garden we were off to a fabulous start.



On Saturday, we were lucky again with warmth and sunshine and so we walked to Airton (of course) for tea and cake. Back at Granny House some slugs realised there were a lot of large logs from a recently felled ancient ash tree on the wrong side of a wall so there was a mad rush of enthusiasm and energy to show each other just how strong we are by throwing bigger logs than anybody else.






The curry night, mainly provided by Anila and Ian, (we shan't mention the rice), was a great success including a very tasty sausage curry and chocolate cake.
Being the last weekend in July, we lit the fire and sat round it a lot.




Sunday dawned a bit wet but we went walking anyway over to Feizor and got a good soaking so back to the fireside to nurse our bruises from yesterday's log whanging with more banter and more food (roast lamb and Nicky's cakes).



Jeanne and Richard wish to thank everyone for their generosity of spirit, the food, wine and beer and the effort put in to driving vast distances to get here.



Dates for your diary:
We shall be making Slugdale cider on Friday 20th October at Granny House and have space for a couple more volunteers, please let Jeanne know.

Phil brought some wood for the bonfire party in November, so we better have another one: Thursday 2nd November to Monday 6th at Granny House".
