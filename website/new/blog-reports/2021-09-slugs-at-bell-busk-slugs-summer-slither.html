 Slugs at Bell Busk

24th/25th July

Slugs Summer Slither.
The weather was kind. Sixteen Slugs enjoyed walking, biking, socialising and eating based at Jeanne and Richard's home in Bell Busk.
On Saturday the main party walked from Otterburn and enjoyed a delightful stroll through one of the quieter parts of Malham Dale. The late arrivals cycled to Settle, Lancliffe, Malham Tarn and back to Bell Busk. All enjoyed an 'al fresco' pizza party in the evening.
Sunday: After a good breakfast we all felt fortified enough to walk around Winterburn Reservoir, another delightful walk, followed by afternoon in the garden and an evening barbeque.
Green travel award: Goes to CJ. Who took the train to Lancaster, cycled to Bell Busk and then returned home by cycling to Clitheroe and train from there.
Hosts of the year award: To Jeanne and Richard who made their home and garden available so that we could spend two evenings nattering (and in some cases) three.
Thanks again to both of them for a great weekend.
