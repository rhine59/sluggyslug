Summer Slither at Granny House

Report posted 30 July 2022 · Published 2022-07-30

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 Summer Slither

Jeanne and Richard opened up Granny House for a Summer Slither.

Early arrivals went for a walk on Friday to Gargrave, Eshton and Haw Crag.

Friday Night was Pizza Night

Bikes always need some fettling before a ride.



Eight Slugs set off biking, with two tandems and various bikes.


Well they said they were biking. Might have been a teashop tour.

Seven walked from Gargrave to East Marton, where they sheltered from the rain in Abbot's Harbour. Before returning by a different route. Elaine, Sue and Ken walked back to granny House, while the others collected the cars.





Saturday saw a much expanded group with both a bike ride and a walk setting off.




Lots of the usual banter and conversation







On the Monday a hardy team explored Dow cave and Dowbergill Passage.
