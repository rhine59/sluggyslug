SLUGS REPORTS

Pre-Christmas get-together at Beetham Cottage, Patterdale

Report posted 21 December 2024 · Published 2024-12-21

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 Beetham Cottage is superbly positioned in-between Brothers Water and the towering Fells of the Eastern Lake District.

We gathered together on Tuesday night and ate a meal together while catching up on News. The rain overnight had abated by 11am when we set out for our walks. Pete, Meg, Chris and Anne to Gowbarrow Fell. Anne, Ken, Colin, John, Kevin and Richard decided to stay as out of the wind as possible and so to  to circumnavigate Place Fell

Descending into Boredale

Over Boredale Hause and into Boredale aiming for Sandwick Bay and the shores of Ullswater

Meanwhile over on Gowbarrow.

There was a bridge to cross.


After lunch we walked back towards the cottage along the shore of Ullswater

We waved to the Gowbarrow crew.

There was no reply because....




By then they were already in a cafe!

Meanwhile Jeanne was busy


Ensuring we had all the Creature comforts when we returned. While we were out walking Ritchie, Martin and Dave Brown arrived.

Fourteen of us sat down for a sumptuous feast. Including Christmas pudding.




Thursday was a much brighter day. Some had already gone home but the rest of us enjoyed a superb days walking.

Stony Cove Pike.

The four seen above plus Ken walked over Stony Cove Pike, Thornythwaite Beacon and High Street





Thornythwaite Beacon.

Descending to Hayeswater and so back to the cottage.

Meanwhile Chris and Anne enjoyed a scenic walk around Brotherwater.




Beetham Cottage in the distance.
We all met back at the cottage for tea and mince pies before making our way home.
