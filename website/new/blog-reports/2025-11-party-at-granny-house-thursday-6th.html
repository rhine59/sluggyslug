 Bonfire Party at Granny House Thursday 6th - Sunday 9th November 2026

A four day extravaganza at Granny House in Bell Busk, this year blessed with fine weather.

Thursday kicked off with Dinner hosted by Jeanne and Richard


On Friday a small party had a walk over Wheets Top to Kirkby Malham where they stopped for lunch in the pub. Before a return leg to Bell Busk.

Wheets Top

Saturday dawned another fine dry day and a larger party walked via Coniston Cold to Gargrave where they enjoyed a picnic lunch by the river Aire.
The walk back to Bell Busk was a delight with the autumn colours looking very fine.

Returning to Bell Busk



The Bonfire had been prepared the week before with a large reserve stack of wood nearby.


Saturday evening was a dry calm night, perfect for standing near the bonfire.


The bonfire was impressive in size.


A willing team of 'stokers' kept it well fed and all the spare wood was burnt.

Sparklers too!

Soon the fire looked like this.


Plenty of big fireworks too.


A great weekend! Thanks to Jeanne and Richard for throwing their home open and hosting the weekend.
