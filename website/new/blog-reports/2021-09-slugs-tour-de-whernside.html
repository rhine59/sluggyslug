 Slugs Tour de Whernside

Saturday 26th June

Twelve Slugs did the ride and Mike walked up Ingleborough. Weather was mostly superb (at least in the afternoon). Lunch stop, cafe stop and finished at the Marton Arms. Lots of lovely riding on quiet well surfaced Dales Roads. Caught a glimpse of a steam train. Thanks to Pete and Meg for organising and everyone for turning up. A great day out.
