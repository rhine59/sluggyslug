Christmas get-together, Buttermere

Report posted 31 December 2022 · Published 2022-12-31

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 Christmas Get-Together Buttermere.

Anne, Ken and Matthew arrived Friday evening, Bill had all ready been in the hut for one day and pronounced it freezing! The Lake District was in the grip of a big freeze and it had been as low as minus 10 every night for a week. He lit the fire and we kept it alight day and night for three days, warming the hut a little.

On Saturday Bill, Matthew, Anne and Ken along with Jes went for a nice walk over Melbreak, made slower by the copious amounts of ice coating the footpath and on the decent, the hard frozen ground. Others arrived at various points during the day and did things like walk around the lake.



Late afternoon was a big get together in the living room. Despite the dire weather forecast and threat of lots of snow on Sunday, 14 Slugs assembled for our annual Christmas Get-Together. After coffee, tea, cake and wine the cooking started.



The kitchen team rustled up a magnificent feast, while others kept the fire going and made a determined effort to reduce the wine lake.



A good meal was followed by flaming Christmas pudding.




Sunday dawned stormy but not yet wet. A determined team set off to climb Haystacks but by the time they got to Warnscale Bothy the wind was proving hard to fight against. We ducked inside for a lunch break and were greeted by fairy lights! Someone was in residence, but they were out.




When we eventually emerged rain had joined the wind, wisdom got the better part of valour and we crossed the icy beck and the descended to the valley. A round of the Lake finished the walk. Meanwhile another team had been inspecting the frozen Sourmilk Gill.



Anne, Ken, Matthew and John stayed Sunday night but it rained hard most of the night and by morning all the ice was gone and strong wind accompanied with copious rain prevailed. After cleaning and tidying the hut we set off home.
