SLUGS REPORTS

SLUGS New Year celebrations

30 December 2023–1 January 2024 · Published 2024-01-03

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 This year the partying kicked off with a celebration for Sylvia and Kevin's significant birthdays.

Party Invite


There was a very well attended ceilidh at Kelbrook Village Hall. Everyone brought food and drink and there was a fantastic spread. The local folk band were very good and everyone got into the spirit with enthusiastic dancing.

Enthusiastic Dancing!


On the 31st there was a walk to a local pub for lunch, near Kelbrook where many Slugs had stayed overnight.

The main event on the 31st was a Curry Night at John's, with the celebrated Riddlesden Curries.

A good turn out with 17 Slugs sitting down to eat and then enjoying the company until midnight.


This year as well as watching the fireworks over Keighley from the vantage point of John's decking we had some of our own, provided by Jeanne and Richard.

Firework Repair Team

Firework Launch Team



Sunday the 1st Jan 2024 was predictably a slow start but eventually we got out for a dry walk!





Through some lovely woods and past reservoirs and then down through the old houses and back to John's.

Tea and an attempt to finish off all the left over food took up the rest of the day.
