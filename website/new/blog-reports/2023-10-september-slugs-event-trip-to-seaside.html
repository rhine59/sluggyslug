September Slugs Event: A Trip to the Seaside!


Cycling around Blackpool, Fish n Chips and Fireworks Saturday 30th September



A multi-faceted range of activity was planned, with different stages that those interested could drop in or out of. In the event, the weather forecast said that it was (very) likely to pour with rain at 3.00pm, the flurry of interest waned, and only Anne Ken and Mike set off from Cleveleys. And even those three hardy souls had every intention of being back at the car by 3.00pm and no intention of hanging around to watch the evening firework display in front of Blackpool Tower – international competition or not!



However, it was a fun and varied 30km ride. First, up the coast to Fleetwood, trying to remember the names of the different shore-birds we were seeing, and discussing the difference between a gaggle and a skein (the area attracting large numbers of geese at this time of year). Soon we came to Pete’s Pool: Fleetwood boating lake, where enthusiasts (such as Pete!) race their radio-controlled model yachts. Stopped to watch and chat…



Not long after, we arrived at the Fleetwood to Knott End passenger ferry where we discovered we’d just missed a crossing by five minutes!






Getting off the ferry in Knott End, our priority was finding the fish and chip cafe that the fliers of microlights and light aircraft go to when they land on Pilling Sands. Mike took us all round Knott End and back again, but the chippy was closed, or else we didn’t find it. Eventually the team enjoyed a pretty good lunch in the cafe by the ferry-jetty.

All this took quite some time, and we suddenly realised we weren’t going to be back by three. Fortunately the rain held off till 3.30. Unfortunately, we didn’t get back to the van until 4.00

A very soggy end to a jolly day out!
