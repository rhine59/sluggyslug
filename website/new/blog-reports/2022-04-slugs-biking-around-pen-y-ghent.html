SLUGS REPORTS

SLUGS biking around Pen-y-ghent

Report posted 6 April 2022 · Published 2022-04-06

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

The team, with Mike behind the camera

With the promise of a dry sunny day ahead, six Slugs slithered into Stainforth. We warmed up with a quick pedal up the road to Horton in Ribblesdale, then the long climb past High Birkwith and Old Ing to Birkwith Moor. 

Climbing Birkwith Moor.

The off-road bit.

Here we left the paved road and tried our hand at some ‘gravel’ riding! This soon changed into forestry track as we passed through the forest at Langstrothdale Chase. Time for a snack-break on those comfy looking white bags. They certainly made soft cushions to sit on, only later did we discover that they contained baby trees! 

From High Greenfield we enjoyed six kilometres of gently undulating tarmac, with fantastic scenery and not a car in sight. The descent of Langstrothdale was even better; lovely Dales scenery and a limestone bedded stream to follow. When we got to the pub at Hubberholme, Mike was already waiting and staring wistfully at the menu. “No!" We said, "Teashop in Buckden!” So off we rode, down Wharfedale. The teashop had closed down so we continued on to Kettlewell, feeling rather thirsty by this stage.

Kettlewell Teashop

Here we found a lovely teashop, with outside seating and a lovely suntrap corner. Coffee and cake made a nice break until Meg announced that we were now at the geographical low point of the ride. There was a big climb to come. We headed up a very quiet Littondale, with again lovely scenery to take our mind off the gradient.
 
Arriving at Halton Gill

Eventually we arrived at Halton Gill, the start of the steep bit. Here the weather decided to have a go at us and it got cloudier, windier and colder the further we climbed.
 
The climb out of Halton gill

Passing Pen Y Ghent wasn’t the usual joy as we were all feeling the cold now. The fast and steep descent to Stainforth was as good as ever but numb fingers spoiled the enjoyment a little. The ride turned out to be nearer 40 miles than the 35 miles advertised but we all made it to the end and enjoyed the wonderful Dales scenery.
SLUGS

Old enough to know better. Still sluggy.
