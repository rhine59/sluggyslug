SLUGS REPORTS

Fifty years of cave exploration below Tresviso

1–8 September 2025 · Published 2025-11-12

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 

Celebrating Fifty Years of Cave Exploration Below Tresviso.



The event took place over the week 1st - 8th September 2025 and timed to run simultaneously with the first week of the Tresviso Caves Expedition 2025. 

For the week of the event thirty one Slugs were present in Tresviso, twenty six of whom had taken part in at least one previous expedition and six had been on the inaugural one. We took over the self catering accommodation and filled the hotel, and there were six camper vans and a tent.

Those who were on the 1975 Expedition. Kevin missing from photo.

The week was mostly spent meeting old friends from the village and making new ones, going for local walks and bike rides, making visits to the Nacimiento (Cueva del Agua) entrance, testing the quality of the food and drink at the two bars and for the more energetic some vigorous mountain biking.

There was also an excursion to visit the Refugio Caseton de Andara, which we used to call ‘The White House’ when it was our base for explorations in the Andara region. During this visit we presented the Guardian with a history of our occupation of the Caseton and a copy of the publication Sima 56.



One of the display boards

Throughout the week there was an exhibition organised by Nick, of photos and surveys of the major caves explored plus photos of village life from 1977 and 1978, the latter proving particularly popular with the Tresvisians.


On Thursday we organised a ‘movie night’ featuring newly edited and digitally remastered editions of films made during 1978 and 1979 by Chris Baxter and Nick Airey. A great success!

At the film show.


The Ayuntamiento of the village had planned a fiesta for the Saturday but they obviously thought we needed some practice first, as they had booked a band to perform outside the Town Hall on Friday night. We turned up, did our best, and danced until the small hours.

On Saturday a full programme of events had been planned and this commenced with an underground cave rescue practice involving members of the expedition and the Servicios de Emergencias del Gobierno de Cantabria. Reopening of the Town hall, Grand Paella at lunchtime, and Mountain Safety talk by Grupos de Rescate Especial e Intervención en Montaña (GREIM) followed.

Programme for the Fiesta.


In the evening there were two bands performing, and the dancing went on even longer than the night before, moving indoors at 1.00am after gusty winds tore down the large canvas roof, which was quickly dismantled by the revellers!

The Mariachi Band


At the Fiesta taken about 3am!


During the celebrations a plaque was unveiled on the newly-refurbished village hall, commemorating fifty years of friendship between the cavers and the village community. For many of us our first visit to Tresviso was our first experience abroad. For the local people (with only a dirt-road for access at that time) visitors from abroad were a rarity. In the intervening years much has changed but the contact has never faltered. We all went home with new memories of fun and friendship, and (as a sign of how times have changed over fifty years) many plans to keep in contact via Social Media in the future.
