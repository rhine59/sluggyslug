 Easegill, Crag Hill and Gragareth

Sunday 16th May

The meeting point was Leck Church car park. We hadn't been walking long when we stopped to admire the fantastic display of bluebells in Spring wood. Continuing along Leck Beck we managed a leisurely stroll, despite Phil's frequent attempts to turn it into a gorge scramble. The scenery and flowers were a delight and I suspect that many of us were still delighted to be able to venture beyond our local walks.



      At the junction with Ease Gill we stopped to enjoy some lunch in the sun. 



Carrying on upstream, soon there was some fun scrambling.



A bit of skinny dipping (pictures on request!) and then more Scrambling.




Returning to our roots, there were plenty of cave entrances to peer into.







A few of us made it down to the top of the first pitch in County Pot while the others lounged in the sun. Below Jes in the entrance shaft of County.




In the upper reaches we headed off up steep ground towards Crag Hill.




While we climbed the weather changed and rain moved in. 




Despite the rain It was still fun and after a while we arrived at the summit of Gragareth. You can see the rain falling in this pic below.





Only the descent to Leck Fell Road left to go.





On the way we met the ''Old Men of Gragareth'
