SLUGS REPORTS

Bonfire party at Granny House

7–10 November 2024 · Published 2024-11-21

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 The event really started the week before with Bonfire building.

Resulting in probably the largest Slugs bonfire ever.





A after bonfire building we felled a tree so that there would be plenty of fuel for the log burner next year.

This involved climbing the tree first!

Thursday evening kicked off with a lovely meal prepared by Jeanne and Richard.

We had to try and determine what the collective term for a gathering of slugs is?


On Friday a small group walked around the classic Malham, Goredale, Janet's Foss walk.




Saturdays walk was Kirkby Fell with a much larger party enjoying the fine weather.




Saturday evening was blessed with dry weather. The bonfire was lit and fireworks plus eating followed.









On Sunday a mellow walk around Airton was enjoyed followed in the evening by a curry night.

OK So this is pizza on Friday night
