SLUGS REPORTS

Pre-Christmas meet at Beetham Cottage

19–21 December 2025 · Published 2026-01-02

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗


When Ken and Anne arrived on Friday Night Martin had already got a good fire going and John was already there. It was a cosy evening with just the four of us by the fire.
Saturday morning and Mike, Richard and Pete and Meg arrived. After tea and chat we set off to Aira Force car park.



After admiring the waterfall we had a very nice walk up to Gowbarrow Fell, the sun coming out occasionally, giving good views and helping us forget the cold wind.




After the summit we stopped for lunch with good views of Ullswater but exposed to the wind, so we didn't linger long.




A superb and scenic traversing path took us back to the car park and cafe, where we all enjoyed a warming drink.
Back at the hut with the fire going again we enjoyed tea and biscuits and some of the history of the hut before the big cooking session.
Seven of us sat down to a good feast with Meg's legendary nut roast and John's traditional mushroom pie. Afterwards Richard treated us to pyrotechnics with his flaming Christmas pud show.



By Sunday's walk we were down to four. Martin had not been up Red Pike so that was our target. At the pass we sheltered behind a wall and the sun came out-it was warm! We lingered in the sunshine. 
Eventually we remembered that Martin hadn't been up Red Pike and started on the long climb.



A quick lunch in the summit shelter, where it was even colder than Saturday. Before we set off down the ridge to Middle Dodd, where we got good views down to Brothers Water.



We managed to find a good path down and had a nice walk back to the cottage for tea and mince pies.
