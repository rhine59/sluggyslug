 Tour of Ingleborough. Sunday 21st April 2024


It was cold when six Slugs gathered at Helwith Bridge on Sunday morning.

We set off anti-clockwise around Ingleborough. The first stop was the Butty Waggon at Ribblehead.

Enjoying a break at Ribblehead

The sun came out and it was very pleasant sitting on the wall enjoying coffee and Yorkshire Tea Bread.

The sun stayed out as we cycled down Chapel Le Dale making it a very scenic stretch with Ingleborough on the left and Whernside on the right. At the church we turned right and got onto the lovely quiet back road under Twistleton Scars. Here we enjoyed the peace and quiet. as well as the sunshine and views.

After a quick whizz downhill, it was steeply up into Ingleton and on past the Old Police Station to Clapham Old Road.

A stop for lunch was made at Newby Cote, where two benches allowed comfortable seating and a view.

Lunch with a view

Next Stop Clapham where we were tempted into The Old Sawmill Cafe for coffee and cakes or soup and a roll. 



The Sawmill has been done up very nicely, with lots of original features retained. 

You can even view the turbine that powered the sawmill using water from the lake and here there is a generator which first brought hydro-electricity to Clapham in 1893. Well worth a visit.


Dragging ourselves away, the sun stayed out through Austwick and back to Helwith Bridge.

In Austwick we pondered what 'Scallywags' are?



Afterwards four of us enjoyed tea and biscuits by the river, in the garden at CPC hut  in Horton.
