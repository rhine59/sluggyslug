 Christmas Meet Borrowdale 13/14th December 2023

We all met up at the Salving House in Borrowdale on Tuesday evening. Wednesday dawned dry and with a good forecast. Two parties set off:

Meg, Pete, Ritchie and Sarah to Whinlatter Forest. They reported

 "A wonderful day on the WOW trail, with tramper hire for Richie."

"It's a recently-opened trail of 4 miles, with super views that WOWed us all.

We ate our lunch in a hide with so many birds & red squirrels to watch having their lunch too."

"A stop at 'sun loungers' enabled Pete to have a well-needed rest! This time the views were the tree tops and clouds scudding by.



The views opened up more"


The final view point really WOWed us all, with Bassenthwaite, Skiddaw range and Keswick before us. And then back to the cafe for a warm drink.


The second party: John, Jes, Martin, Anne and Ken walked up Great Crag, starting from the hut.
The sun came out at lunchtime


Great views from the summit.



Warm enough to enjoy  tea and cake outdoors back at the nut.



Wednesday evening we ate the banquet, prepared by talented Slugs. Dave and Mike joined us for the evening. Now we were eleven.



Thursday: Long slow breakfast, followed by chatting.



After most had set off for home, Mike, Anne and Ken went in search of the Graphite Mines.



We found one and Mike was pleased to find an actual piece of wad. He didn't take it home because that would be punishable by 5 years in the Gaol!
SLUGS
