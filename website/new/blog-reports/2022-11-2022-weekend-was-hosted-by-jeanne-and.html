Bonfire 2022 at Granny House

Report posted 15 November 2022 · Published 2022-11-15

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 Bonfire 2022

The weekend was hosted by Jeanne and Richard and Granny House as usual.

Folk started arriving on Thursday (you have to be early to bag a bed!)

On Friday we walked from Gargrave to Bank Newton and back with lunch at Abbot's Harbour.

Lunch at Abbott's Harbour.

Saturday was a walk along the River Ribble from Lancliffe to Stainforth, near Stainforth Bridge we hoped to see leaping salmon. After years of hopefulness we actually saw 6 leaping Salmon.

Meanwhile ten salmon are leaping behind us!


On the return leg we called in to see the very impressive Hoffman Kiln near Lancliffe Quarry.

Hoffman Kiln at Lancliffe

Inside the Hoffman Kiln

Bridge over the Ribble

Saturday night was bonfire night, with lots of food and drink to enjoy as well as a huge bonfire and loads of fireworks.

Warmer ourselves by the bonfire.




Sunday was a leisurely walk from bell Busk to Airton, with lunch at Town End Farm.


Also of course lots of socialising and chatting.



 
We were all able to welcome Kamla, Martin, Tony, Penny, Mike Airey and Aga, Matthew and Megan to their first Slugs Bonfire party. Jim and Paul were very special guests having not been seen for many years.
Sue and George, Ian and Liz, Simon, John, Rosie and Phil, Richie, Dave and Nicky, Anne and Ken, Dave and Linda, Rob and Annie, Kevin and Sylvia, Mike, Howard M, Phil and Elaine were all spotted at some point during the weekend.

 Jeanne and Richard wish to thank everyone who helped with meals, coffees, bonfire building and management, fireworks, lights, lanterns and clearing up outside and inside.

Everyone would like to thank Jeanne and Richard for their superb hospitality.
