SLUGS REPORTS

Ski ’23, Alpe d’Huez

Report posted 10 February 2023 · Published 2023-02-10

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 Ski '23 Alp D'Huez


Last year was still a bit dodgy for the covid-cautious, so the “official” Slugs trip was put on hold, and the booking carried over to this year. Now, in ‘23, the enthusiasm was buzzing. The original 13 participants were all still keen, the 14th bed was snapped up, and a second apartment for 6 was duly booked and filled.

Hence a total of twenty arrived in Alpe D’Huez, with a whole range of experience, skills and energy between them. If you wanted to get up late, stop for coffee, or come back early, you’d find a willing companion. If you wanted to get out on the first lift, eat your lunch in the bubbles, and squeeze in the last run of the day, you wouldn’t be the only one. Between us we cruised the blues, aced the blacks, and adventured in the off-piste (not to mention the shopping and tourism!). Good weather and good snow too, so all-in-all a great trip.

Many thanks to Kevin for organizing and coordinating, and finding good value spacious accommodation (complete with swimming pool, sauna and steam-room)



Communal meals were varied and veggy


The apartment was big and quiet enough for relaxing and chatting... Kevin has GoPro footage from the swimming pool, but you're not going to see it here!



With the wonders of modern technology (an app called Maprika) it was easy to check out where other Slugs were skiing, or whether they were lingering over lunch!

Great snow, and plenty of sunshine...

.... just the occasional snow shower to keep the pistes fresh!

And having mentioned shopping and tourism;



Jeanne bought new boots, and Jes visited the Ice Cave. (Don't know what this is though!)
