A Rum Do: Isle of Rum

Report posted 11 July 2022 · Published 2022-07-11

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 A Rum Do



On a fine sunny Saturday 11 slugs set off on a voyage to the Isle of Rum. With the fine sunny weather we could admire excellent views of the Skye and Noydart hills. 



The skipper directed the boat past a school of dolphins and cut the engine so we could watch them feed and jump etc.



On Rum we settled in to our various chosen accommodation, camping, pods, Harbour Hut and for the greatest comfort the Bunkhouse.



With the weather being so good on the first day Anne, Ken, Matthew and John took the opportunity to walk the famous Rum Cuillin. First a 10km ride to Harris Bay.



Followed by 32km walk over the 8 summits, with grand views of Skye to finish the day. 



Everyone else with Bikes visited the lonely Mausoleum at Harris Bay and Kilmory, with only one injury on the rough tracks.

Good weather continued. The most popular peak to climb were Barkeval, 



followed by Hallival and Askival (the highest). Other popular walks were to the bothies Dibidil in the south East and Guirdil in the west. 



Beaches were enjoyed and wildlife spotting. 


One morning Martin disappeared off on his bike with a fishing rod and later came back with two fine trout for supper, part of his eight fish bag. 



The fashion accessory of choice was the Midge Net, although really the midges weren't much of a problem (although the campers may disagree!)




Simon showed us some of the fascinating and varied geology of the island.



Thursday the weather started to deteriorate and Friday was a very windy day most of us escaped on the ferry a day early as there was a risk the ferry would not run on Saturday. 




Kamla, Martin and Simon bravely stayed on and escaped by the skin of their teeth in a force 7 gale, with all ferries cancelled after their voyage.

A great trip enjoyed by all.
