 Weekend at Raw Head Cottage in Langdale

Team Photo before the rain came

With an ever changing cast, as folk came and went, we had eleven Slugs spread over three days and managed an enjoyable and sociable time despite the weather.

Friday evening six of us gathered for a communal meal and talked long into the night beside the flickering flames of the fire.

By the fire


Having passed a very cold night we were up reasonably early on Saturday morning. However, by the time we had all got our systems working, our breakfast done and taken some team photos it had started to rain.

Woods above Elterwater

Anne, Ken and Richard set off to walk to Skelwith Bridge via Elterwater. Along the way they marvelled at how many trees had been blown down in the recent storm. Reaching Skelwith Bridge they enjoyed coffee and cake and a quick sandwich in the shelter of the take-away's awning, before enjoying the walk back to Raw head. Being pretty wet when they got back it was straight into the drying room for them.

Biking above Elterwater, one of the many fallen trees


Meanwhile Kevin and John had further delayed their bike ride with more tea and prevarication. Once they did set of  they enjoyed (?) a very wet ride around the Tilberthwaite area, finishing in the dark.

By now Howard and Ritchie had arrived and we were all sitting by the fire enjoying Howard's healthy parkin and getting down to some serious nattering.

Communal meal Saturday Night.

With the arrival of Jes our numbers reached their maximum at nine souls and we all enjoyed a convivial communal meal with a good variety of dishes plus Christmas Pud and cream for afters.

Walking in Langdale


By Sunday morning Jeanne and Richard had departed but Pete and Meg arrived. We all enjoyed a bracing walk up to the vicinity of Angle Tarn. We couldn't see the tarn because the cloud was down at ankle level, but we could sense it's presence. A wet stomp back was followed by eating up the leftovers before we tidied and cleaned the cottage and set off home.

A good weekend and well worth the effort. Thanks to all who came along.
