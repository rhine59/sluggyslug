Tour of Howgills : a re-cycle event,  16th October

Sat 16 Oct saw 7 SLUGS gather together in Sedbergh, in the gloom and heavy drizzle. For most (except Pete) it was ‘3rd time lucky’ after being unable to make the original re-arranged August cycle ride. The usual ‘SLUG-ish’ start paid off ( time taken to pump tyres, don waterproofs, etc), ensuring that within 20 mins of uphill cycling the skies brightened and precipitation ceased… so off came the waterproofs!


Obligatory bike fettling


The team minus John and Nikki

We started to have tantalising, atmospheric glimpses of the Howgills. Further clearing allowed a spectacular sighting of a steam train on the line towards Tebay. A closed road, due to the annual British Fell Relays event, prompted a short walking section. The long, downhill section back to Sedbergh was a particular joy!


Lunch stop

 Refreshments of a roadside packed lunch, drinks at a riverside beer garden and coffee back in Sedbergh all kept us well-fuelled. 


Riverside beer garden




Many thanks to those who joined us, making it a great day out.

Meg.
