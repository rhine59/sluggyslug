 Slugs Weekend at Loftburn

18th/19th September

Thanks to Mike and Anila for a great weekend at Loftburn. Eleven Slugs plus three well behaved dogs turned out on Saturday.
After tea and cake on the lawn. We had a delightful walk up Pin Haw in superb weather with superb views.
On the way back we called in at The Bull in Broughton for drinks on the terrace.
Later we enjoyed a superb meal and the usual enthralling conversation and drinking.
Those who stayed over Saturday night had a comfortable sleep on the lawn (in Tent).
On Sunday we were treated to Mikes 'chest high stinging nettle walk' followed by the promisingly named 'Bog lane'. later still we wandered around the residential areas of Skipton staring at our mobile phones trying to find where Rob and Annie live. When we finally found them we were revived by a cup of tea.
More aimless wandering was avoided by Rob kindly offering to give us a lift back to Loftburn. Convenient as a rain shower had just started.
Thanks once again to Mike and Anila.
