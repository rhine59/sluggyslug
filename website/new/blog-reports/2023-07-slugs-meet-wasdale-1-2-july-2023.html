SLUGS REPORTS

SLUGS meet, Wasdale

1–2 July 2023 · Published 2023-07-08

← Events · Blog Archive · Original blog post ↗

 On the Saturday Anne attempted the Joss Naylor Challenge.



In short running 48 mile across The Lake District, visiting 30 summits and with 17 000 feet of ascent.



Ground support was provide by Ken, Mike, Linda and Dave. Anne had 4 teams of support runners, mostly from Clayton Harriers Running Club but also Matthew ran one leg with her as did Abbie from the Craven Pothole Club.



Linda and Dave dropped her off at Pooley Bridge just before 4am, where she met her first two support runners.

They met her again 3hr 22 mins  later at Kirkstone Pass for the first food stop and change of support runners.





 Ken met Anne and her support runners at Dunmail Raise (10.05 am), the first two legs of the run had been wet and windy and it was three very wet but smiling ladies who arrived at the van for pints of tea and snacks. 



The following leg was dryer but more windy, with the wind knocking the runners off their feet at times and Anne acquired some minor injuries to her legs. This pic may give you an idea how windy it was.



Ken next met Anne, Matthew and Nikki at  Styhead Pass (3.10pm) after a 50 minute dash from Wasdale, where he feared he might be too late.



Dave and Linda provided food and drink at Black Sail Pass.



Anne was raising money for North West Air Ambulance and just by chance as she approached one pass the NW Air Ambulance was landing to pick up a casualty.


Anne completed the challenge arriving at Greendale Bridge Wasdale after 16 hours and 27 mins at 8.27pm despite fierce opposition from the weather. On the The final leg they had encountered wind, rain and hail showers and the team of three took quite a battering.



She had set a new record for women over 65 beating the previous record by 13 mins.

On the Sunday Anne was resting and eating but four intrepid slugs set off to visit Burnmoor Tarn.



The weather was better than on Saturday and we had a pleasant walk to the tarn, with only one short shower on the way. We investigated Burnmoor Lodge, which was in the process of being renovated.



On the way back we had one or two showers but also sunny intervals and enjoyed very good views back to Wasdale. At Wasdale we had tea and cake in the the Fell and Rock Club hut, Brackenclose before Pete and Meg set off for home.
