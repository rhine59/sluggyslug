Mountain Biking from Sedbergh, April 2011

After two cancelled events in a row we finally got one off the ground. The planned ride over the Howgills was easily abandoned and we all went for a pleasant ride over to Dent for a sunny lunch. A visit to Whernside Manor was made for old times sake, then a trip along Abbey Road! Up, Up, Up and onto the slightly wet tops. A very pleasant ride along the ridge followed by a fun descent and we were back in Sedbergh by 5pm.
 
A great day out.
