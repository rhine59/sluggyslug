A little history.

The Society of Lancaster University Graduate Speleologists: old enough to know better, and still sluggy.

CLAPDALE · 1976

“There are no rules.”

SLUGS was inaugurated in the warden’s room at Clapdale during the Bar Pot weekend in 1976. The constitution said, “there are no rules”.

And so it goes on: friends, caves, hills, shared weekends and a growing collection of memories.

Thanks to the people who kept the stories

Ian Edmundson, for creating the original website. Paul Simm, for hosting its pages. George Shaw and Sue Ianson, for creating—and finding again—the Tresviso 2000 website.

The photographs and writing remain credited to their original contributors.
